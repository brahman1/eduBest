## Proposition de notes pour Apple, à adapter après résolution des accès

EduBest is a companion app for the EduBest school in M’Chedallah, Algeria. Students and parents may submit an admission request. Email confirmation and school approval are required before account activation. The app supports French and Arabic. Timetables use the Africa/Algiers timezone and financial records are displayed in Algerian dinars. The app records and displays school fee transactions; it does not sell digital subscriptions or process bank card payments in the app.

Student, parent, teacher and administrator access is role-based. The account deletion request is available in Account > Delete my account for eligible personal accounts; some school financial records may be retained separately as explained in the privacy notice. No advertising identifier or advertising tracking is used.

The production app has no public demonstration switch. Review login instructions: [COMPLETE ONLY WITH WORKING, ISOLATED ACCESS VERIFIED IN THIS BUILD].

Ne pas soumettre ce texte avec le champ entre crochets ni affirmer qu’un compte est isolé sans l’avoir vérifié.
